## Main historical project facts
- **AI Predictive Modeling of Neuron Interactions (2024):** supervised research in the High-Tech Systems track, TU/e Honors Academy. Hypothesis: activity of a neuron may be predicted from surrounding neurons using deep learning. Focus on temporal and spatial interactions and comparative models. The archive provides no final numerical results; do not invent any.
- **Bioelectrical signals and mental states (2022 research project):** investigated whether bioelectrical signals can help identify and potentially predict mental states in response to stimuli. Archive credits consultant Zlatogor Minchev (Bulgarian Academy of Sciences) and mentor Zvezdin Besarabov (UCL). The exact formal project title is not documented; use a descriptive non-claiming title.
- **RoboBuba (early project, exact year NOT established by archive):** educational/research robot; Bluetooth wireless control using accelerometer/gyroscope, environmental measurement and recording, multiple physical versions; 3D printed and designed with Fusion 360. Good legacy engineering case study.
- **QUINN (early project, exact year NOT established by archive):** personal assistant that recognizes emotion in spoken commands and can react; combined ML, speech, and expressive robotic hardware. The archive does not specify accuracy for QUINN itself; do not transfer all results from the separate emotion-recognition experiment without explanation.
- **Voice Emotion Recognition (early research, exact year NOT established by archive):** studied classifying eight emotions from voice recordings. The old page reports 93–94% (female model), 66–67% (male model), and 87% (gender-neutral model); these are historical reported figures with unknown testing protocol in this archive and MUST NOT be used as generalization claims. Strongly prefer caveated, qualitative summary unless verified against the original paper.

## Context & problem

RoboBuba was designed as an educational and research robot, bringing together electronics, motion control, environmental sensing, and physical fabrication in a platform for learning.

## My contribution

I designed and 3D-printed the robot body using Fusion 360 and developed multiple hardware iterations. The project combined an Arduino-based robot with Bluetooth control using accelerometer and gyroscope input, including an accelerometer glove described in the archive.

## Technical method

The robot supported wireless motion control and environmental measurement and recording. Three versions allowed the physical design and assembly to evolve. The educational intent favored components that could help students learn common IoT and robotics techniques.

## Results & lessons

The original images document three physical iterations. The About Me archive also records using RoboBuba to teach robotics to children, connecting the prototype with hands-on education.

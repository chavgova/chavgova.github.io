## Context & problem

The project investigated the hypothesis that a neuron’s activity can be predicted from the behavior of surrounding neurons. The problem connects deep learning with the spatial and temporal structure of biological neural activity.

## My contribution

I conducted individual deep learning research within the High-Tech Systems track of the TU/e Honors Academy. I implemented and compared RNN, CNN, spiking neural network, hybrid, and Transformer models for neural activity prediction, and proposed an informed modular model.

## Technical method

The research considered spatial relationships between neurons and temporal relationships in their activity. Comparing models was central to the question of how these dependencies could support prediction.

## Results & lessons

The current CV source reports that the proposed modular approach outperformed the other models in the project’s comparisons. This is a within-project result, without supplied scores or a documented evaluation protocol; it does not establish superiority beyond those experiments.
